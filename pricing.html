Your Quote

What affects your price

Your starting price can change based on a few things:

  • How many transactions you have each month
  • How many bank, credit card, and payment accounts you use
  • Whether your books are up to date or need catch-up work
  • Whether you need payroll, sales tax, invoicing, or bill pay
  • Extra complexity, such as inventory, multiple locations, or more than one business

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Why “starting at” prices?

Every business’s books are different. Starting prices give you an honest range up front, and the consultation lets me quote a price that fits your actual workload, so you never pay for more than you need.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. All monthly packages are month to month. You can cancel any time with 30 days’ written notice.

What if my books are behind?

No judgment, it’s very common. Catch-up work is quoted separately as a one-time project, and then your regular monthly package begins.

Can I change packages later?

Yes. As your business grows or changes, you can move up or down a package.

What software do you use?

I’m a QuickBooks Online ProAdvisor. If you’re using something else, we can talk about switching during your consultation.

Do you prepare tax returns?

I don’t prepare income tax returns, but I’ll keep your books clean and send a year-end package to your CPA or tax preparer so tax time goes smoothly.
