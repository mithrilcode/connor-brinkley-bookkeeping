FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you work with clients remotely?

Yes. Most of my work is done securely online, so I can work with you wherever you’re located. We can meet by video call, phone, or in person when possible.

How much do your services cost?

Pricing depends on the size and complexity of your books and the services you need. After a free consultation, I’ll give you a flat-rate quote so you always know what to expect.

What accounting software do you use?

I work with QuickBooks Online, Xero, and Wave. If you don’t have software yet, I’ll help you choose and set up the best fit for your business.

I’m months behind on my bookkeeping. Can you help?

Absolutely. Catch-up and cleanup projects are one of my specialties. I’ll get everything reconciled and up to date, then keep it that way.

How do I send you my documents securely?

I use an encrypted client portal for document sharing — never plain email attachments for sensitive information like Social Security numbers or bank statements.
