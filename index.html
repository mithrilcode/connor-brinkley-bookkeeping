FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you prepare or file tax returns?

I focus on bookkeeping. I don’t file tax returns, but I keep your books clean, reconciled and organized all year, then give your CPA or tax preparer exactly what they need. That usually makes filing faster and less expensive.

Do you work with bars and restaurants?

Yes, it’s my specialty. I managed a bar for more than four years, so I understand tips, POS systems, inventory, vendor bills and the cash flow swings that come with hospitality.

Do you only work with San Antonio businesses?

I’m based in San Antonio and happy to meet local clients in person, but bookkeeping works great remotely too, so I can help businesses anywhere in the U.S.

How much do your services cost?

Pricing depends on the size and complexity of your books and the services you need. After a free consultation, I’ll give you a flat-rate quote so you always know what to expect.

What accounting software do you use?

I specialize in QuickBooks Online and I’m an Intuit-certified QuickBooks ProAdvisor. If you don’t have software yet, I’ll help you get set up the right way.

I’m months behind on my bookkeeping. Can you help?

Absolutely. I’ll get everything reconciled and up to date, then keep it that way.
