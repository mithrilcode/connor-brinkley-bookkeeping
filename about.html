About Me

Hi, I’m Connor Brinkley.

I’m a San Antonio bookkeeper who loves turning messy numbers into clear, organized books — and I’ve spent years on the other side of the counter, helping run the kind of small, local businesses I now serve.

  • San Antonio native
  • QuickBooks Online Certified
  • Former bar manager

My Story

I’ve always loved making the numbers make sense.

Some people dread spreadsheets. I’ve always found real satisfaction in taking something disorganized and putting it in order — every transaction in the right place, every account reconciled, and a clear picture of where the money is going. Bookkeeping lets me do that every day, in a way that makes a real difference for business owners.

I was born and raised in San Antonio, and most of my career has been spent working at small, local businesses here in town. I know how much owners juggle, and how easy it is for the books to fall behind when you’re busy serving customers. That’s exactly the problem I want to solve.
